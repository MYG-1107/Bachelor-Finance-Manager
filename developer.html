Engineering & Architectural Foundations

A high-contrast, zero-telemetry financial reconciliation web platform.

Bachelors Finance Manager resolves the common problem faced by university students and bachelor flatmates: complex, multi-party expense sharing across variable monthly bills (such as room rent, electricity meter readings, groceries, water cans, and internet).

Built strictly with pure JavaScript, semantic HTML5, and Scandinavian design principles (adhering to Norwegian NAV.no and UDI.no usability patterns).
